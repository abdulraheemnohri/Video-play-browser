# Privacy Policy for VIDEOPlay Browser

**Last Updated: September 2, 2026**

Thank you for using VIDEOPlay Browser. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app.

---

## 📌 Information We Collect

### **1. Information You Provide**

VIDEOPlay Browser does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to use the app. However, you may choose to:

- Save bookmarks
- Save browsing history
- Save download history
- Save video watch history

All of this information is stored **locally on your device** and is **not** sent to our servers or any third parties.

### **2. Information Collected Automatically**

When you use VIDEOPlay Browser, we **do not** automatically collect any of the following:

- Your IP address
- Your device information
- Your location
- Your browsing history (unless you explicitly save it)
- Your search queries
- Any other personal information

### **3. GeckoView Telemetry**

VIDEOPlay Browser uses **Mozilla's GeckoView** as its browser engine. By default, GeckoView may collect some telemetry data to help Mozilla improve their products. This data is **sent to Mozilla, not to us**.

You can disable GeckoView telemetry in the app settings if you prefer not to share this data with Mozilla.

---

## 🔒 How We Use Your Information

Since we do not collect any personal information, we do not use your information for any purposes. All data is stored locally on your device and is under your control.

---

## 🌍 Data Storage and Security

### **Local Storage**

All data you save in VIDEOPlay Browser (bookmarks, history, downloads, etc.) is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data.

### **Security**

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your locally stored data:

- **Encryption**: Sensitive data may be encrypted on your device
- **Biometric Lock**: You can protect access to the app with fingerprint or face recognition
- **Clear Data**: You can clear all browsing data at any time from the app settings

### **Data Retention**

You control how long your data is stored:

- You can clear your browsing history at any time
- You can delete individual bookmarks or downloads
- You can clear all app data from your device settings

## 🤝 Third-Party Services

### **GeckoView (Mozilla)**

VIDEOPlay Browser uses GeckoView, which is developed by Mozilla. Mozilla may collect telemetry data to improve their products. Please refer to [Mozilla's Privacy Policy](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/privacy/) for more information.

### **Android Services**

VIDEOPlay Browser may use Android system services such as:

- **Download Manager**: For managing file downloads
- **MediaSession**: For media playback controls
- **Picture-in-Picture**: For background video playback

These services are provided by Google and are subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 📜 Your Choices

### **1. Clear Browsing Data**

You can clear your browsing data at any time:

1. Open VIDEOPlay Browser
2. Go to Settings → Privacy → Clear Browsing Data
3. Select the data you want to clear
4. Tap "Clear Selected Data"

### **2. Disable Telemetry**

You can disable GeckoView telemetry:

1. Open VIDEOPlay Browser
2. Go to Settings → Privacy
3. Disable "Tracking Protection" and other telemetry options

### **3. Use Private Browsing**

You can use private browsing mode to avoid saving history:

1. Open a new private tab
2. Browse as usual - no history, cookies, or cache will be saved

### **4. App Permissions**

You can control app permissions:

1. Go to your device Settings
2. Find VIDEOPlay Browser
3. Tap Permissions
4. Enable or disable permissions as needed
